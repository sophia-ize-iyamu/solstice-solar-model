# Investment Committee memo: Project Solstice

**Asset:** 200 MW utility-scale solar + 50 MW / 200 MWh storage, North America
**Ask:** approve $80.5M equity commitment | **Total capex:** $230M (65% geared)

## Recommendation
Proceed. The project clears an unlevered IRR of **9.5%** and a levered equity IRR of
**14.9%** (5.59x MOIC) on a 20-year contracted PPA, with a minimum
DSCR of **1.94x** that holds the debt comfortably through the amortization period. Returns
sit in the band expected for contracted renewables, and the merchant tail past year 20 is
upside, not underwriting.

## Key value drivers and risks
- **PPA price and term** are the dominant value driver: the contract underwrites ~20 years of
  cash flow, after which the asset runs merchant. The sensitivity below isolates PPA price and capex.
- **Capex** is the other swing factor; procurement timing on modules and batteries moves the equity IRR.
- **Resource and degradation:** a 25% capacity factor declining 0.5%/yr.
- **Refinancing / merchant tail:** years 21-30 are modelled at a conservative
  $42/MWh; a contracted extension is upside.

## Decarbonization impact
Displacing grid power at 0.40 t CO2/MWh, the asset abates about **175 kt CO2/yr**
and **4.9 Mt over its life**, an implied **$47/tonne** on capex alone.
This maps to a transition-investment framework that scores both return and abatement.
